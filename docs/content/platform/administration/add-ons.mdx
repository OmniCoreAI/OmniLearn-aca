# Add-ons

Add-ons are optional (or required) extras offered with training — meals, printed material, training kits, equipment rental. Each add-on has a category, price, currency, tax rate (included or added), unit (per participant, per session, per day, flat), an availability window, optional stock, an image and a status.

## Attaching add-ons

Attach add-ons to a course or training program, optionally with a different price, as required or optional, and with a maximum quantity. Courses that used the old per-course add-on list were migrated into the catalog automatically.

## Participant choices

Learners see an **Optional add-ons** card on the course page and choose quantities. Each choice records the price and tax at that moment. The **Participant selections** tab lists every choice with totals and exports them to CSV.

Selections are recorded only: no payment is taken and no finance ledger entry is created.
