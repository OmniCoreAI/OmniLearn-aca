# Instructors & Rates

An **instructor** extends a platform user with a teaching profile: bio, areas of expertise, languages, weekly availability, a photo and a category.

## Categories and rates

Each **instructor category** (for example *Senior Trainer*) has a base hourly rate, optional per-language rates, and a currency. The rate used for a session is chosen in this order:

1. the instructor's own **rate override**, if set (e.g. category 500 EGP, instructor 650 EGP → 650);
2. the category rate for the session's delivery language;
3. the category base rate.

Work logs keep the rate they were created with, so changing a rate never rewrites past pay.

## Adding instructors

- **Existing user** — pick any member of the academy.
- **New person** — enter a name and email; an account is created with a one-time password and the Instructor role.

The instructor page shows the rate breakdown, availability, the courses they teach and their work logs. Courses are assigned from the instructor page or from a course's **Delivery & Resources** tab.

## Instructors proposed by entities

When an entity's coordinator is allowed to propose instructors, the proposal appears with status **Pending approval**. The academy approves it, choosing the category and (optionally) a personal rate; the person then gets the Instructor role. Coordinators never see categories or rates.
