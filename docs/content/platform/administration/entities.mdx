# Entities & Coordinators

An **entity** (الجهة) is an organization the academy trains for — a ministry, government body, university or company. Entities are *not* separate tenants: they live inside the academy and share its courses.

## Creating an entity

Give the entity a name (English and Arabic), code, type, optional parent entity, contact details and logo. Each entity automatically gets an **all members** group, which is maintained by the platform and cannot be edited by hand.

Tabs on the entity page:

| Tab | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| Overview | Details, coordinators and what coordinators may do |
| Members | People, with their position, employee ID, groups and status |
| Groups | Departments and other groups inside the entity |
| Positions | Positions specific to this entity (shared positions are managed under **Positions**) |
| Instructors | Instructors linked to or proposed by the entity |
| Available & assigned learning | Courses and programs made available to the entity and who they are assigned to |
| Progress & activity | Enrollment, progress, certificates and last activity per member and per course |
| Imports | Excel imports of members |

## The Entity Coordinator (منسق الجهة)

A coordinator manages **only their own entity**. Make someone a coordinator from the entity's Overview tab, choosing an existing user or creating a new account. Trainees get platform role 2, **Entity Coordinator**; academy admins keep their own role.

Coordinators work from **My entity** in the sidebar. What they can do is switched on per entity:

- add, edit and deactivate members;
- import members from Excel;
- create groups and choose their members;
- assign the training the academy made available to the entity (to the whole entity, a group, a position or individual members);
- propose instructors, if the academy allows it (off by default). Proposed instructors stay pending until the academy approves them.

Coordinators can always review their members and follow their progress. They cannot see other entities, academy settings, finance, instructor rates or the academy-wide user list.

<Callout type="warning">
Role 2 used to grant academy-wide administration. It is now limited to entities. Move any academy staff who held role 2 to **Academy Admin** before upgrading.
</Callout>

## Assigning training to audiences

On a course's **Access** tab (and on a training program page), **Assign to audiences** lets you choose entities, user groups, positions (optionally within one entity), cohorts or individual people:

- **Assign** restricts the course to those audiences (plus staff). Options: enroll them automatically, notify them, and a due date.
- **Make available** (entities only) grants nothing yet: it lets that entity's coordinator assign the course to their own people.

Assignments are applied through the existing user-group access, so they follow membership changes: new members, a changed position or a deactivated member update access automatically. A training program's audiences also reach every course in the program. Inactive groups keep their links but no longer grant access.

<Callout type="info">
Assigned members can open a course only once it is published. Public courses stay open to everyone; switch the course to **Users only** for assignments to restrict access.
</Callout>

## Importing members from Excel

1. Download the template — it has dropdowns for the entity's positions and groups. Headers may be in English or Arabic.
2. Upload the filled `.xlsx` or `.csv` file (up to 5 MB and 5,000 rows).
3. Review: every row is checked (required fields, email format, duplicates in the file, employee IDs, phone numbers, positions, groups, existing accounts). Nothing is created yet.
4. Confirm: new accounts are created and existing ones linked, members join their groups, and a course made available to the entity can be assigned to everyone. New accounts receive a link to set their password.
5. Download the rows that need fixing, correct them and import them again.
