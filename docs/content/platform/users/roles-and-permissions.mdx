# Roles and Permissions

OmniLearn uses a role-based access control model. Roles are stored as database rows with a per-resource permission matrix (courses, users, usergroups, collections, organizations, chapters, activities, roles, dashboard, boards, playgrounds), so organizations can customize permissions or create their own roles on top of the defaults.

Every new instance is seeded with four default global roles:

## Admin

Admins have full control over the organization. They can:

- Manage all courses, collections, and content
- Manage users and assign roles
- Configure organization settings (branding, AI, signup)
- Access all platform features

## Entity Coordinator (منسق الجهة)

Role 2 (historically *Maintainer*, then *Organization Coordinator*) is the **Entity Coordinator**. It has learner rights plus access to the dashboard, and manages **only the entities it coordinates** — never the whole academy. Coordinators can:

- Add, import, review and deactivate their entity's members
- Create their entity's groups and choose their members
- Assign the courses the academy made available to their entity
- Follow their members' progress
- Propose instructors, when the academy allows it (the academy approves them)

Which of these are allowed is set per entity by the academy. See [Entities & Coordinators](/platform/administration/entities).

<Callout type="warning">
Before this change, role 2 had academy-wide administration rights. When upgrading, move academy staff who held role 2 to **Admin**; the upgrade resets role 2's permissions and its sidebar to *Home* and *My entity*.
</Callout>

## Instructor

Instructors focus on teaching and creating educational content. They can:

- Create and edit their own courses, chapters, and activities
- View learner progress for their courses
- Use the AI assistant for content creation
- Manage collections they own

Instructors cannot modify organization-level settings or manage other users' roles.

## User

Users are the default learner role. They can:

- Browse and enroll in available courses
- Complete activities, assignments, and quizzes
- View their own Trail and progress
- Interact with the AI assistant for learning support

Users cannot create or edit courses or access administrative features.

## Custom Roles

Admins can create additional roles (global or organization-scoped) and fine-tune each permission individually from the dashboard.
